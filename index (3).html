Rooted in ritual

A salon that moves at your pace

Maison Belle is a small, appointment-only studio with five private stations and a treatment room overlooking an interior garden. We keep our schedule deliberately light so no one is rushed and no chair is shared.

Our stylists, colourists and aestheticians train in classical technique and use professional-grade, cruelty-free products, chosen for how they feel on skin and hair, not for the label.
